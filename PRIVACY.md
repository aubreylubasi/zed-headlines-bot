# Privacy Policy – Zambia News Bot

_Last updated: November 2026_

This app is a private tool used by the owner of one Facebook Page to publish links to
Zambian news stories on that Page automatically.

**What it accesses.** Only the Facebook Page owned by the app's administrator, and only
to create posts. It does not read, collect or store information about any Facebook user,
follower or visitor.

**What it stores.** A list of news article links it has already posted, so it does not
post the same story twice. No personal data is stored.

**Sharing.** No data is sold or shared with anyone.

**Data deletion.** The app holds no user data. To remove the app's access to the Page,
go to Facebook → Settings → Business integrations and remove the app.

**Contact.** For questions, message the Page this app posts to.
